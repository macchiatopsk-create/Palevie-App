# PALEVIE content production operating rules

## Purpose
Convert PALEVIE from "a stream of text-over-color PowerPoints" into a beauty-first account demonstrating real action. Keep the existing Shade Battle control, but never assume other static-graphics Reels will work.

## Human sign-off mandatory
INSIGHTS → CONCEPT → SOURCE FOOTAGE → ROUGH CUT → PHONE SCREENING → RIGHTS/COPY REVIEW → OWNER APPROVAL → SCHEDULE → 24h / 72h POST-REVIEW.

Do not claim "posted", "published", "Instagram scheduled", "verified skin accuracy" or "successful product trial" without evidence. The CLI preflight status only allows the *manual* scheduling stage; it never publishes.

## Reel acceptance criteria
- First two seconds visually show the hook, before any logo/title sequence.
- Beauty-real and beauty-pov videos show *actual filmed action*: hand applying product, genuine makeup movement, authentic close-up product handling, or reaction footage. A slideshow/AI character pretending to apply cosmetics is insufficient.
- 9:16; 5–60 seconds; captions readable in mobile safe zones. Watch the final render on a phone.
- Commercial usage rights, model/subject release where applicable and sound-use rights must be documented privately.
- A stock model wearing makeup is not a PALEVIE customer endorsement.
- Screen demos show the current PALEVIE product, with accurately named features. On-device guide is a light/dark contrast aid, not validated personal-color diagnosis.
- Color/product visuals are illustrative unless exact shade evidence exists. No "100% accurate", fabricated match scores, unsupported price or medical claims.
- One natural CTA per Reel. Test with sound on and off.
- Record Instagram engagement and website visitors as separate metrics.

## Weekly cadence with quality stop
Start with three visually different finished Reels: 1) real lip application, 2) hands-on makeup or POV, 3) real site recording. Shade Battle remains a measured control.

If all three lack genuine motion, rights or owner review, **do not scale**. Fix production first. An experimental 5–6/day schedule is considered only after 3 finished cuts pass quality and are explicitly approved.

## How to evaluate
Connected Metricool reports views, reach, likes/comments/saves/shares and average watch seconds. Compare at equal post ages (24h and 72h), note sample sizes, and don't call watch time a retention percentage. Site attribution and human interaction are independently measured. Account exports stay private.
