## The current customer flow at palevie.com/quiz
- Default is the previously polished **seven-question Quick Color Quiz** with short multiple-choice answers, result palettes and Amazon Shop exploration.
- A quick quiz starting palette is guided by self-reported features and makeup preferences. **It is not a scientific determination of a user's personal color season**. Do not market it as one.
- A visible **Camera scan / Live color guide** path is available during the quiz and above quiz results, or users can select **Mirror**.
- The guided camera compares **Soft/Bold lightness contrast effects** with on-device video and color overlays; it does **not** autonomously confirm warm/cool season or diagnose a face. Its optional "Start scan" live score remains a beta styling aid. It must never be described as validated personal-season inference.
- The optional **Advanced 7-color face comparison** at /quiz?mode=expert offers more structured warm/cool, value and chroma observations and tentative four-season direction only if internally consistent; it is not required to finish the quick quiz.
- No subscriptions, premium upsells or user payment. Amazon product links only to separately reviewed exact identities; prices and availability are confirmed on Amazon.
